## 3. THE SCIENCE — the central question is answered
### POSITION, not values (§41)
Every burst episode captured is **the correct data at the wrong place**. Quiet frames match the
simulator at offset 0 at **100.00 %**; burst frames match at non-zero offsets at **100.00 %** —
all 12,320 symbols, both rails.

### One ladder, seven rungs (§42, §43)
| rung | offset (sym) | fraction | seen at tap 3 as |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | 6176–6432 | 0.5013–0.5221 | plain displacement |
| 6–7 | 6489, 6548 | 0.5267, 0.5315 | **I/Q rails SWAPPED** |

Three instruments — 1 Hz DBGCAP, FEC-anchored `cap_in`, raw DDR — agree on the same rungs **to the
symbol**.

### Geometry and rate (§43)
Jump to **half-frame + 16 symbols**, then a walk of 372 symbols (3.0 % of a frame) at a measured
**≈3.9–4.2 ppm** — three independent estimates. Hypothesis (labelled as such): a discrete re-lock
to half a frame, then a few-ppm clock-rate walk, then recovery. **No offset between 0 and 6176 has
ever been observed.**

### Two limits on the above, both found by testing (§45, §46)
- **§45: the measurement cannot say WHICH side moved.** Everything is data measured relative to
  the demod frame marker, which cannot separate "data arrived late" from "marker fired early". The
  second (FEC) marker does not help — the two are **exactly coincident in every capture**, quiet
  and burst, because the RTL derives FEC `startIn` from demod `startOut`. Discriminating needs a
  TX-side reference in the same capture, and this instrument captures **one selector at a time**.
  Read the headline as *"data and frame marker are separated by ~0.5 frame"*.
- **§46: the ladder is verified and the residue is CLOSED.** Across every archived 1 Hz run
  (92 non-golden tap-3 words), plain offsets are exactly the five known rungs, swapped exactly the
  two, and **no offset between 0 and 6176 appears anywhere** — the half-frame jump is discrete. The
  only two leftovers are torn reads (zero LOW byte, the MSB-first partial-fill signature).

### The beat is at least THREE phenomena (§44)
1. displacement; 2. displacement + I/Q swap (one-symbol inter-rail skew — points at I/Q
interleaving or the rate-change handoff); 3. **heavy errors with a bit-perfect demod input**
(46,907 errs/s, 100.00 % match) — **errors arising DOWNSTREAM of the demod input.** First episode
localised to a stage.
