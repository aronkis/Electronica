# Task 12b — R4B: the silicon-ready form of R4 (structural guard window, kit-applicable anchors, registered path, bounded pre-fill, witnesses), sim cut + gate

## Why (read task-11-report.md, task-12-brief.md, and the adversarial review of the R3S gate summarised in the ledger at 17:1x)
The adversarial review of R3S (3 refuters + critic) did not refute the headline result (2 of 3 not refuted, high confidence; the third refuted only the residual accounting and wording), but it found design facts that make R4 as cut unshippable on silicon:
1. **The guard window is "deframer idle", not "the 13-slot inter-frame gap".** `guardIn = ~sample_discard_controller.active` opens on ANY idle period: after a false sync, a missed sync, a filler or garbage frame. One of 240 R3S skips fired mid-payload (s_m40 air frame 138, tref 7026, after a false Preamble_Detector sync) and that frame died. On silicon 5–6 % of air frames are garbage/filler at the decoder pins, and R4's predicate (occ ≤ 8) is true almost always at steady state (ring parked at 8/9), so R4 would skip mid-payload often. **R4B fix:** the skip window is STRUCTURAL: a one-shot window of exactly 13 enb-domain valid slots opened by End_Generator's `pcEnd` (the dump shows pcEnd at rel −4 and the R3S skip at +3 inside the guard), i.e. skip allowed only in slots [pcEnd+1, pcEnd+13], at most one skip per pcEnd, and NEVER on sdcAct-low periods that are not preceded by a pcEnd within 13 slots.
2. **Injector anchors do not reach the packaged IP kit:** tests 73/91 pin that R3S/R4 anchors fail on the `TxRxCompo_ip_src_`-prefixed module names the kit uses; W1 solved this with the `_PFX` pattern. **R4B fix:** `_PFX`-tolerant anchors for every R4B hunk + a test that applies W1+R4B to a kit-shaped tree (prefixed modules, both zip members, verify_zip) and to the --sim-tree.
3. **Timing:** R4's pop expression closes a combinational loop `Delay_out1 (occupancy) → compares → do_skip → pop → valid_pop → count → Delay_out1` and pulls `guardIn` through four hierarchy levels combinationally. **R4B fix:** register the window flag and the `≤ 8` / `≥ 16` compares (one enb tick of latency on the skip decision is harmless; the window is 13 slots wide); no combinational path from occupancy to pop other than the baseline guard.
4. **Fail-to-silent pre-fill:** **R4B fix:** `r4b_prefilled` also sets after 4096 enb ticks post-reset regardless of occupancy (bounded release), with a test.
5. **Lock definition:** counting guardIn falling edges under-counts in merged-frame regimes and counts false syncs. **R4B fix:** lock = 8 `pcEnd` pulses since reset (same structural signal as the window).
6. **Witnesses for silicon:** expose `{r4b_prefilled, r4b_armed, r4b_skips[15:0], r4b_window_opens[15:0]}` as a NINTH W1 read word at 0x234 when W1 is present (add the word to the W1 variant's read mux in a way that leaves the eight existing words untouched; if W1 is absent the witnesses stay internal). Update two_jup/rxfix/W1_REGMAP.md with a W1+R4B section and the changed at-rest expectations (occupancy after arm ≈ 16 on non-burst arms, up to 31 on burst arms; pop_on_empty after arm may be 0 — the liveness positive control for R4B is r4b_prefilled = 1 and r4b_window_opens advancing at the frame rate).
7. **Threshold:** keep pre-fill 16 / skip at occ ≤ 8 unless Task 12's R4 gate (running now; results in two_jup/comb/sro_sim/ as r4_* files) shows a reason to move them; state the reason if you change them.
