## Headline

**The 32.4-frame loss comb is not caused by the valid-density hole.** It is caused by the
receiver's sampling phase jumping by **32 symbols (128 input samples)** once per SRO
symbol-slip and back one sample-slip period later. Peak_Search and Timing_Adjust follow the
jump correctly; the frames that sit at the displaced offset decode fine; **the two frames
that straddle the change are the loss**, and 2 frames per cycle reproduces the measured
4.11 % (−2.5 ppm) and 23.04 % (−10 ppm) exactly.

Two fix variants were cut and gated. **Both fail, and their failure is the finding**:

| variant | what it does | gate |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** valid-indexed realignment-FIFO pop | removes the only tick-counted valid in the receive chain (`Delay10_reg`, 49,332 enb ticks) | s = 0 byte-identical; **−2.5 ppm 4.11 %, −10 ppm 23.04 %, lost indices identical to baseline frame for frame → NO EFFECT** |
| **R2** frame-sync flywheel on Peak_Search's argmax | tracks the locked peak in a ±2-symbol window, rejects the displaced one | s = 0 byte-identical; **−10 ppm 34.80 % vs baseline 23.04 % → WORSE**, and the newly lost frames are the excursion frames themselves |

R2's failure was **pre-registered in the ledger while the leg was still running**, and it
also serves as a free control that refutes the "tiled stimulus makes any offset hash the
same" objection: if a shifted extraction hashed identically, R2's held-offset frames would
still have been OK. They turn CORRUPT. The displacement is real.

## Localisation of the +32

| evidence | conclusion |
|---|---|
| per-beat ring dump, ±64 beats around `pop_on_empty`: pointers step by 0/1 only, occupancy 0…2, longest `validOut` gap 9 beats (nominal 4), wrap branches never taken | the Rate_Handle ring is **exonerated**; the EMPTY edge is a clean single-slot hole; **lap replay excluded** |
| per-local-frame valid census at `ss`/`cfc`/`cs`/`pd`/`corr`: 12,333 on 409 of 417 frames, 12,332 on 8, 12,365 never, surplus 0 | **no symbols are inserted or deleted** anywhere in the chain |
| `pdPof` = `pd_pop_on_empty` = 0 on every leg (T0a) | the Preamble_Detector realignment FIFO guards are **excluded** |
| correlator magnitude + threshold at every beat with `tref` ∈ [0,90]: threshold flat to ±0.5 %, magnitude at the true position collapses 277.6 M → 1.77 M, the same magnitude appears at +32 | **the peak MOVES**; not a competitor, not a threshold artifact |

| per-strobe dump over frames 37.00-42.07 (the whole excursion): 62,500 strobes = 12,332.6/frame uniform, spacings only 3/4/5 samples, none > 8, no `countReg` half-field jump | the **interpolator strobe / basepoint / NCO wrap is excluded** |

So the displacement is measured beyond doubt, and **every structural mechanism proposed so
far is now excluded by direct measurement.** No R3 was cut: there is no localisation left to
cut it at.

A final probe measured the displacement **in the symbol data** rather than through the
correlator (one row per `Rate_Handle.validOut` over frames 37-42, CFO-invariant differential
symbols, mean-removed and normalised):

- a **content transient is not indicated** — per-frame mean amplitude and phase are
  essentially unchanged through the excursion;
- consecutive-frame cross-correlation has **two peaks of nearly equal height**, ρ = 0.50 at
  lag 0 and ρ = 0.50 at lag ±32, everything else at ρ = 0.02;
- and that double peak is present in **every** frame pair, including non-transition ones.

**That double peak is withdrawn as a lead — it is a binning artifact.** The stimulus is one
TX frame tiled, so consecutive frames are identical and a correct correlation would give
ρ ≈ 1.0 at lag 0 alone; two equal 0.50 peaks is the signature of slices cut on input-sample
boundaries containing a rotated splice of the tile. The TX frame's own differential
autocorrelation settles it: ≈0.18 at every integer-symbol lag, 0.004 at non-integer lags, no
lag-32 preference in the transmitted content.

So there is no surviving mechanistic lead — only the measured fact of the displacement.
