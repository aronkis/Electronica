## 3. WHERE THE INVESTIGATION STANDS (this is the important part)

### The question, and the part that is answered
Periodic ~239.5 s "beat" defect. The central question was **position vs. values** — is the data corrupted,
or is correct data landing in the wrong place?

**It is position. [SILICON]** §57 classified every error-carrying frame in a 4.37 s capture: 90 of 98 are
piecewise position sequences; no frame demonstrated corrupted values. §53 confirmed **jumps, not walks**,
on a pre-registered test — zero intermediate offsets in 13,000+ frames.

### Where the displacement is — the localisation as it stands
Chain: raw in → AGC → RRC → **symbol sync** → coarse freq → carrier sync → preamble detect → demod → FEC

| tap | stage | status |
|---|---|---|
| sel0 | raw input | **UNTESTED** |
| sel1 | AGC out | **WITNESS-DEAD** (§72) — tap produces nothing |
| sel2 | RRC out | **INDEX-DEAD** (§75) — data alive, marker anchor unusable |
| sel3 | postSymbolSync | **DISPLACED** [SILICON] (§72) |
| sel4 | postCoarseFreq | displaced |
| sel5 | postCarrierSync | **DISPLACED** [SILICON] (§69) |
| sel6 | demod in | displaced |

So: **everything from symbol-sync output downstream is displaced, and every stage from there to demod is
transparent** — it passes the displacement through without creating or removing it. The fault is at or
upstream of symbol sync. The three upstream taps are all unusable with the current instrument, which is
why the localisation stops exactly there.

### §73/§74 — the open lead
There is a **third stable state at sel3 that has no counterpart at sel5** — not aligned, not displaced by
any known rung, not rail-swapped. Reproduced on one arm (§74). Because sel5 is clean of it, it is
plausibly *sub-symbol/fractional* and gets resolved by the downstream stages. Testing whether it exists at
RRC output would have discriminated "exists before symbol sync" from "created at symbol sync" — and that
is precisely what §75 could not do.
