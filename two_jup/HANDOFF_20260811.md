## 2. WHERE THE FAULT ACTUALLY IS

Attribution moved twice, each time because a measurement overturned an inference. Final
position, with the evidence:

### The bit errors are made in the DEMODULATION/DECISION path
Four Tap-A IQ captures with EVM in 100 ms windows aligned to the `biterr` timeline, one
**triggered on the bit-error rise itself** (5.8x baseline):

| capture | across the onset | Δ EVM |
|---|---|---|
| blind rep 1 | flat, BER ~2.8e-5 | — |
| blind rep 2 | clean 14.95% vs degrading 14.87% | **−0.08 pp** |
| blind rep 3 | flat 14.94–15.33% | — |
| **triggered** | clean 14.93% vs degrading 14.96% | **+0.03 pp** |

Threshold for "signal quality" was fixed at >1.0 pp **before** the data. Comparisons are
**within a single capture** — same gain, same LO, seconds apart.

Two independent arguments, not one:
1. EVM does not change across the onset.
2. ~15% EVM **cannot** produce 2.8e-5 BER from constellation noise at all — QPSK at that
   EVM sits nowhere near its 45° decision boundaries.

The constellation is clean and the bits are wrong.

### Everything upstream eliminated by direct measurement
| subsystem | evidence | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| AGC / RF level | `agc_level` pinned at 12 before/during/after | not it |
| SSI / ADC delivery | `maxGap` median 1, no pre-cursor | not it |
| carrier resets | `rstcs` no pre-cursor | not it |
| carrier loop | `cfc` flat until **5.3 s AFTER** errors start | **consequence, not cause** |
| LO offset | null vs +20 kHz: TTW 20.3 vs 20.2 s, golden 97.0 vs 97.2% | not it |
| channel/algorithm | float oracle recovers 161/161 frames, ceiling 0.0000% | not it |

Loop-gain retuning is a **closed avenue**: a 4x range across `cs_prop_gain` and
`cs_integ_gain` was indistinguishable once wedged, because it sits downstream of the
trigger. There IS a real stability cliff between `cs_prop_gain` 98→196 (biterr 461 →
14668/s, reproduced at 300), so the default has only ~2x margin — worth hardening on
design grounds, but it is **not** the episode trigger (`csprop49` doubled the margin and
wedged identically).
