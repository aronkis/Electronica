## 1. THE HEADLINE — the campaign's central question is ANSWERED

**POSITION, not values.** During bursts the frame anchor is displaced by
**0.50–0.53 of a frame**, and the symbol values at the displaced position are **bit-exact**
against simulation. Confirmed on **two instruments with different anchors, windows, packings and
units**, agreeing to four decimals. (§31, §35)

Supporting structure:
- The burst visits a **closed set of 7–8 states** that does not grow with more sampling, and
  **reproduces across reset cycles** hours apart (§25, §27).
- Within a burst the displacement **only increases** — a progressive slip, not a jump (§32).
- The two burst families (the old "BIG sml" alternation) visit **interleaved** rungs; bursts start
  every ~117 poll iterations, and the 235 recurrence is two burst periods (§32).
- Every rung sits at **half a frame plus an integer number of capture windows** — three of five are
  exact integers on both instruments. Suggests a frame-sync mis-lock to the half-frame ambiguity
  plus a slip. **Hypothesis, not a result.**

### Read this before quoting any burst percentage
`0x20C` holds the last completed frame — **one frame sampled per second out of ~1272**. Golden
captures coexist with 39k–73k bit errors. **Every golden-constancy burst figure is a lower bound
on displacement, not a duty cycle** (§32).
