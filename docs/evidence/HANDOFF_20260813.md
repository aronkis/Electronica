### THE NAMED DIVERGENCE (pin-level, from system_top_routed.dcp of all three)
In BOTH witness builds, Vivado synthesis RE-HOSTED the modem's rail-generation
and byte-plane enable logic OUT of the DUT into the AXI-Lite ADDRESS DECODER
hierarchy -- the exact module the witness overlay extended (0x1C4/0x1C8 reads):

| fact | e49c011b (works) | fsv2 AND wit3 (both ~46%) |
|---|---|---|
| tc cells as routed | **100** (phase pair dissolved into a tc-local 100-LUT CE-gating bank) | **4** (bare phase_0/phase_1 + LUT1) -- IDENTICAL in fsv2 and wit3 |
| enb_1_2_0 driver (60.8-60.9k loads) | `u_TxRxComposite_tc/phase_0_reg_0[0]_BUFG_inst` (BUFGCE, I = tc-local AND-LUT) | `u_TxRxCompo_ip_addr_decoder_inst/data_reg_axi_enable_1_1_reg_1_BUFG_inst` (BUFGCE, I = **addr-decoder-hosted** AND-LUT) |
| post-synth enb_1_2_0 driver | LUT in `u_TxRxComposite_tc` | LUT in `u_TxRxCompo_ip_addr_decoder_inst` -> **the restructure originates at SYNTHESIS**, not P&R |
| byte-plane gated enables | tc/module-local | canonical drivers hosted in the addr decoder with names from the BYTE PLANE: `state_bitIdx[5]_i_1__0/O` (**ByteSerializer** bit-step) -> enb_1_2_0_gated FO=21; `dataOut_last_value_i_1/O` (**sample_discard_controller**) -> enb_1_2_0_gated_0 FO=210 |
| gated-enable globals | ONE gated global (FrameStatFifo enb_1_2_0_gated, FO 3236) | TWO gated globals (1503 + 1067) + `_alias`/`_gated_N` fragmentation across hierarchies |

Trigger mechanism: the witness stall counter wires byte_rx_valid/byte_rx_ready
into new logic that is ALSO read back through the addr decoder (0x1C4), giving
cross-hierarchy resource sharing a bridge between the byte plane and the AXI
region; Vivado then elected addr-decoder copies as the canonical hosts for
shared enable LUTs design-wide. e49c011b's netlist has no such bridge and keeps
everything DUT-local.
